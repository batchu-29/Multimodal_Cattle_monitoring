# Where the sample images came from

`samples/` and `out/` are test material for the demo, not original work. Check the licence of
each source before reusing anything outside this project.

## out/ (generated by the scripts in this repo)

Frames, overlays and contact sheets made from third-party material:

- the "Dairy Cow Gait Scoring Video" by Janice Siegford on YouTube (id `dyjscNt1WzM`, described as material for a university course), cows 1-5: `gait/`, `ref3/`, `t_cow4/`, `t_cow5/`, `cow45_sheet.jpg`
- two YouTube Shorts of Indian cows (ids `lnTaXHgrGlk` and `pbTG7Mmd0D8`): `short1/`, `t_street/`, `t_white/`
- the lame-cow photo above: `lame1/`
- `records/`: two test records read back from Firestore (a frame from the gait video and its pose overlay)

The pose overlay (green dots, yellow lines) is drawn by `gait_detection.py`.

## Model weights

`yolo11n.pt` is Ultralytics YOLO11 nano weights (AGPL-3.0), used by `frame_extraction.py` to find cows.
